Four small promises.

Click a file, or use the arrow keys and Enter. The desk works the same with a pointer or a keyboard.

  1. 01

    Read the review.

    The line above the files is the whole assignment for that round.

  2. 02

    Mark only bugs.

    Yellow notes and clean files cost a review. The word on the cell is the label, not only the color.

  3. 03

    Keep three reviews.

    They sit as hexes under the timer. Spend them slowly. The third one closes the desk.

  4. 04

    Stamp it merged.

    Three rounds make a pull. On the later pulls the files swap, then the marks start to move.
